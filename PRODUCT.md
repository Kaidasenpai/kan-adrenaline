# Adrenaline Tasks

## Register

product

## Users

Adrenaline Entertainment's internal team of 10–13 people manages projects,
events and production tasks. Requests currently arrive through WhatsApp and
verbal instructions, with spreadsheets used when tracking becomes difficult.

## Product Purpose

Give the team a shared record of task ownership, deadlines, progress and
approvals at task.adrenaent.com. Keep the deployment within the existing free
hosting plans and support practical coordination through WhatsApp.

## Brand Personality

Professional, clear and confident. Use Adrenaline Entertainment's established
identity and the existing application components.

## Anti-references

The user explicitly rejected a cheap, generic visual treatment. Avoid confusing
actions, buttons that imply unavailable features, and unnecessary paid-service
prompts in this internal deployment.

## Design Principles

- Make each action describe the result it actually delivers.
- Preserve work and valid access links when an operation fails.
- Show progress and prevent overlapping submissions.
- Use realistic event and production workflows.
- Keep routine team actions straightforward on phones and computers.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support natural Arabic and English, right-to-left layouts, keyboard operation,
clear control names, visible focus and useful error messages. Preserve the
existing brand tokens and responsive app shell during focused fixes.
