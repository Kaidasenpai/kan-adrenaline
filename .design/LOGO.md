# Adrenaline Tasks identity

The final identity extends the supplied Adrenaline Entertainment logo: the original offset-square symbol and custom stacked ADRENA / LINE letterforms, with a small TASKS descriptor. Exact native artwork is retained in separate transparent symbol and wordmark assets and colored with theme-aware CSS masks. App/install icons use the original symbol.

The earlier square/check concept was rejected and removed from the app. The image-generated redraw was not selected because its letterforms and alpha edges were inferior to the supplied original. Generated high-fidelity screen references inform the implemented workspace and sign-in design; these are in references/. Generation prompts are recorded in GENERATION-PROMPTS.md.

Brand colors: #B4D234 lime, #171A15 ink, #F5F5F2 canvas, white. Interface type: Plus Jakarta Sans. No proprietary fonts were extracted from the company profile.

Production assets: apps/web/public/branding/adrenaline-symbol.png and adrenaline-wordmark.png. Shared component: apps/web/src/components/BrandLogo.tsx.
